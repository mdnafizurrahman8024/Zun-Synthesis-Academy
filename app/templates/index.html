    Privacy & Security Policy

    Zun Synthesis Academy is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our learners.

    Data Collection

    We may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, learning interests, and messages, to respond to enquiries, manage registrations, and deliver educational services. We collect only information relevant to these purposes.

    Data Security

    We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

    Cookies

    This site may use browser storage, including localStorage, to remember your theme preference. Essential cookies or similar technologies may support site functionality. You can manage or clear these through your browser settings.

    Third-Party Integrations

    We use third-party services for fonts, icons, and potential sign-in or embedded features. Those providers may process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

    Anonymous Visit Analytics

    We record the page path and server-side visit time to understand aggregate traffic. We do not collect IP addresses, cookies, browser fingerprints, or account identifiers for this feature.

    Your Rights

    You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have shared with us, subject to applicable requirements. Contact us using the details on this page to make a request or ask a privacy question.

    This policy may be updated as our services evolve.

    About The Founder

    Md. Nafizur Rahman

    Hello! I am a dedicated Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) student at the Department of Veterinary and Animal Sciences, University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh. My academic journey is built upon a strong foundation in Anatomy, Histology, Physiology, Pathology, Parasitology, Microbiology, Medicine, Surgery, Epidemiology, and Public Health.

    I am deeply passionate about bridging the gap between biological research and advanced computational science to improve animal welfare, public health, and disease prevention. Throughout my journey, I have integrated traditional veterinary science with modern Data Analytics, developing proficiency in Python, SQL, GeoPandas, and Matplotlib to drive evidence-based research.

    Clinical Surveillance

    Working on a 'One Health' project evaluating veterinary prescribing patterns, owner KAP, and self-medication in companion animals.

    Nanomedicine

    Evaluating nanoparticle-based Rabies vaccines and nanoparticle remedies for heavy metal bioaccumulation in livestock.

    Epidemiology

    Extensive studies on ectoparasites in dogs, Capillaria spp. in poultry, and bedbug risk factors in student residences.

    Wildlife Rescue

    Active animal welfare advocate, participating in snake rescue with SWAN and providing free clinical services for stray animals.
